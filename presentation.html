Part 6: Complete Mathematical & Chemical Appendix

1. Higher Heating Value (Dulong's Empirical Formula)

$$HHV = 0.3383C + 1.442\left(H - \frac{O}{8}\right) + 0.0942S$$

Variables: $HHV$ is gross enthalpy ($\text{MJ/kg}$); $C, H, O, S$ are elemental mass fractions of Carbon, Hydrogen, Oxygen, and Sulfur.

Detailed Explanation: The coefficient $0.3383$ represents the heat release from carbon combustion. The term $\left(H - \frac{O}{8}\right)$ isolates free hydrogen; oxygen bound to hydrogen in a $1:8$ mass ratio is pre-oxidized as water and yields no thermal energy.

2. Lower Heating Value (Net Usable Energy Output)

$$LHV = HHV - h_{fg} \times (MC + 9H)$$

Variables: $LHV$ is net enthalpy ($\text{MJ/kg}$); $h_{fg} \approx 2.26 \text{ MJ/kg}$ is latent heat of vaporization; $MC$ is moisture content; $H$ is hydrogen mass fraction.

Detailed Explanation: Subtracts latent heat consumed converting moisture and combustion-formed water into steam ($1\text{ kg}$ of Hydrogen burns to form $9\text{ kg}$ of $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ vapor).

3. Second Law Exergetic Efficiency

$$\eta_{\text{ex}} = \frac{\dot{E}_{\text{out, syngas}}}{\dot{E}_{\text{in, biomass}} + \dot{E}_{\text{parasitic}}} = 1 - \frac{T_0 S_{\text{gen}}}{\dot{E}_{\text{in}}}$$

Variables: $\eta_{\text{ex}}$ is exergetic efficiency; $\dot{E}$ is exergy rate; $T_0 = 298.15 \text{ K}$; $S_{\text{gen}}$ is entropy generation rate.

Detailed Explanation: Measures useful work potential. Minimizing entropy generation ($S_{\text{gen}}$) maximizes chemical energy conversion quality beyond simple First-Law heat conservation.

4. Zero-Slagging Silica Temperature Constraint

$$T_{\text{reactor}} \le 850^\circ\text{C}$$

Detailed Explanation: Rice husk contains $\approx 15\text{--}20\%$ silica ($\text{SiO}_2$). Enforcing a strict upper temperature ceiling below the Ash Fusion Temperature ($850^\circ\text{C}$) keeps silica as dry ash, preventing glassy slag formation and reactor fouling.
